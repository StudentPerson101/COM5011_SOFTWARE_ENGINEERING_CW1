4. Concise Process Summary
A sound strategy for FASAM is:
 - Elicit and organize requirements from the scenario and stakeholder input.
 - Validate them using reviews, scenario walkthroughs, simulation, and hazard analysis.
 - Remove ambiguity, resolve conflicts, and define measurable acceptance criteria.
 - Baseline the approved requirements.
 - Manage them in a version-controlled repository with full metadata.
 - Apply formal change control for all modifications.
 - Maintain end-to-end traceability from source to requirement to design to test.
 - Re-validate and re-baseline when safety- or behavior-critical changes occur.


5. Conclusion
The supplied FASAM requirements form a solid initial set for a safety- and security-critical monitoring system, particularly around alarm monitoring, escalation, evacuation, logging, and containment. However, because the domain is high-risk, validation must go beyond simple review and include scenario analysis, conflict checking, hazard analysis, and testability refinement. Requirements management should be formal, change-controlled, and fully traceable so that every behavioral rule can be justified, implemented, tested, and audited throughout development.
